Option A — Design Decisions

Card
Rate badge moved top-left
Eye enters top-left in LTR scanning. Rate seen early without competing with the face, which should stay dominant.
Card
Name is the anchor, role secondary
Hirers identify candidates by name first, then confirm role fit. Previous layout made role and name equal weight.
Card
Hover reveals "Quick Preview" strip
Makes the card's click affordance explicit. Words "Quick Preview" bridge card → modal, reducing surprise.
Card
AI score chip top-right
Quality signal should be visible at a glance in the grid, before the user decides to click. Amber colour flags it as a score, not a category.
Modal
Left panel: video resume, not static photo
RemoteRecruit's core differentiator is video resumes. Showing the same photo twice (card + modal left) added no new information. The video panel gives the modal a distinct job.
Modal
Avatar in detail panel reuses same face
Continuity: user recognised the face on the card. Small avatar beside the name in the modal confirms "same person", removing any disorientation.
Modal
AI Score as bar, not just a badge
A yellow badge with "AI Score: ⚡" told users nothing about whether 72 is good or bad. A filled bar gives instant proportional context.
Modal
CTAs stacked, Message full-width
Message is the primary conversion goal. Equal-width side-by-side CTAs split attention. Stacking with Message larger reinforces hierarchy without hiding "View profile".
Modal
Rate label above value (not below)
Label → value mirrors natural reading order. Previous layout had value first, requiring the eye to backtrack to understand what the number meant.
